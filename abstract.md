## **Abstract**
Organizing social gatherings often involves the complicated process of coordinating availability and preferences across group chats, schedules, and forms. This creates tedious work for friends, family, students, and community organizers who want to plan gatherings, resulting in many events being delayed or not even happening. Conflicting schedules and differing preferences make finalizing event details challenging, which creates an opportunity for a planning platform with all of these features integrated which the average person can use to easily plan the events that they care about.

Our objective is to create an application that simplifies planning for small, casual hangouts and is also scalable to larger gatherings. We envision the app to have both a web and mobile application where one or more hosts can invite guests, collect time availability, and also gather dietary restrictions, budgets, and activity preferences. From these responses, our app will then suggest activities and times for the function, creating a shared event page with details and RSVPs once the host confirms. This application will be an improvement over the current field where many avalability schedulers, invitation platforms, and form services exist since our solution centralizes all of these features into one place. Our advantage is that by converting all of these tasks into one simple workflow, we will reduce the friction that exists for event organizers.

We will judge our project based on user evaluation from event planners to measure the improvements that our app has compared to their existing workflows and whether it improves the quality/quantity of their gatherings.
